# Billing

Billing tracks your organization's plan, usage, and subscription. Every organization starts on the free **Developer** tier, and you upgrade as your fleet grows. Plans set your member, device, tunnel, claim-token, and tunnel bandwidth limits.

- **Current plan and usage**: See how many members, devices, tunnels, and claim tokens you're using against your plan's limits
- **Upgrade** from the **Plans** tab
- **Payment methods and invoices**: Manage them in the billing portal

## Plan limits

| Plan         | Members            | Devices         | Concurrent tunnels | Claim tokens   | Tunnel bandwidth |
| ------------ | ------------------ | --------------- | ------------------ | -------------- | ---------------- |
| Developer    | 1                  | 1               | 1                  | 2              | 1 Mbps           |
| Startup      | Per purchased seat | 20              | 5                  | 10             | 5 Mbps           |
| Teams        | 2, expandable      | 100, expandable | 25                 | 10             | 5 Mbps           |
| Professional | 25                 | 500             | 25                 | 50             | 20 Mbps          |
| Enterprise   | No fixed limit     | No fixed limit  | No fixed limit     | No fixed limit | No fixed limit   |

The Developer tier is free and sized for one developer with one device. Tunnels on the Developer tier can't be extended past their initial lifetime. Startup is a paid plan for the first 12 paid months: when it ends, the organization moves back to the Developer tier unless it upgrades to Teams. On Teams, you can add seats and devices beyond what's included from the **Plans** tab.

Enterprise organizations have no fixed limits. Enterprise isn't available from the **Plans** tab: to discuss it, [contact Peridio](https://www.peridio.com/contact).

The **Plans** tab always shows your organization's current limits. When you reach a limit, Connect blocks the action, for example enrolling another device or sending another invitation, until you upgrade or free up capacity. If you need more on your current plan, an owner or admin can submit a limit increase request from **Settings → Limit Requests**.

## Manage your subscription

Go to **Settings → Plans** to see your current plan and usage, and to upgrade. Subscribing opens a secure checkout, and **Manage billing** opens the billing portal, where you update payment methods and view invoices. Every member can see the **Plans** tab, but only owners and admins can change the subscription.

The billing endpoints are `GET /api/orgs/$ORG_ID/billing` for the current plan and usage, `POST /api/orgs/$ORG_ID/billing/checkout` to start a subscription, and `POST /api/orgs/$ORG_ID/billing/portal` to open the billing portal.
