# Groups

Groups are organization-wide collections of people that you grant access to projects and cohorts in one step. They organize people, not devices: your engineering team, your QA team, or your field technicians. To group devices, use [cohorts](/avocado-connect/deployments#cohorts) and tags.

- **Create named groups** and add organization members to them
- **Grant access in bulk**: Give a group access to a project or cohort instead of adding people one by one
- **Inherit access automatically**: A member added to a group gains everything the group can access
- **Revoke in one place**: Remove a member from a group to take away every access it granted

## Common patterns

- `engineering`: Access to every project the team builds.
- `qa`: Access to the staging cohorts it tests, but not production.
- `field-ops`: Access to the production cohorts it supports in the field.

## How it works

Go to **Groups** in the console, click **Create Group**, and give the group a name (up to 100 characters) and an optional description. Open the group and use **Add Member** to add people who are already members of your organization.

To grant the group access, open a project's or cohort's settings and turn off **Organization-wide access**. The **User & Group Access** section then appears, where **Add Group** gives the group access. While organization-wide access is on, everyone with access to the project can already reach it, so group grants have no effect. Cohort access also requires access to the cohort's project.

Connect checks group membership on every request, so membership changes take effect immediately. Owners and admins can reach every project and cohort without a group. Only they can create groups, manage members, and grant group access.

With the API, create a group and add a member by user ID:

```bash
curl -X POST "https://connect.peridio.com/api/orgs/$ORG_ID/groups" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $AVOCADO_TOKEN" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"name": "qa", "description": "QA team"}'

curl -X POST "https://connect.peridio.com/api/orgs/$ORG_ID/groups/<GROUP_ID>/members" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $AVOCADO_TOKEN" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"user_id": "<USER_ID>"}'

curl -X POST "https://connect.peridio.com/api/orgs/$ORG_ID/projects/<PROJECT_ID>/access/groups" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $AVOCADO_TOKEN" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"group_id": "<GROUP_ID>"}'
```

Grant a group access to a cohort the same way, at `/api/orgs/$ORG_ID/projects/<PROJECT_ID>/cohorts/<COHORT_ID>/access/groups`.

See [Access Control](/avocado-connect/access-control) for how group grants combine with direct grants, and the [Administration API](/developer-reference/avocado-connect-api/administration) for every group endpoint.
